Privacy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This website

schnobb.ca is a few static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms or accounts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security as described in its privacy statement. I don't have access to those logs.

Email I send

Email from schnobb.ca is transactional: messages that a person or a system asked for, such as account verification, password resets and service notifications for the projects I run. I don't send marketing email or newsletters.

What I collect

To send those messages I keep the recipient's email address and, where the project needs it, a display name. I use them only to deliver the mail that was requested and to run that project.

I don't sell this information, share it for advertising, or add anyone to a mailing list.

Who processes it

Mail is delivered through Zedmail, which processes the recipient address and message content in order to send it, and records delivery events such as bounces and complaints. Addresses that bounce or complain are suppressed so they are not mailed again.

How long I keep it

For as long as the account or service the mail relates to stays active. Once it is closed, or you ask, the address is deleted.

Asking to be removed

Email privacy@schnobb.ca from the address in question, or tell me which address it is, and I will delete it and stop all mail to it. You can also ask what I hold about you or have it corrected. I reply within a few days.

Who is responsible

Chris Schnobb, an individual in Ottawa, Canada. More on the About page.
