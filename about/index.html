About

I'm Chris Schnobb. I live in the Ottawa area with my wife and kids, and online I usually go by MrAlders0n.

Day job

I'm an IT security consultant. For over a decade I've helped government and enterprise organizations protect their most critical systems and data, mostly in high-assurance environments.

My work centres on secure network architecture, secure remote access, zero trust, and security monitoring. In practice that means working with the people who own a system to agree on what needs protecting, designing something practical, and making sure what gets deployed matches it.

Mesh

A work engagement introduced me to RF, and I got curious. That led to LoRa and to MeshCore, a way of building radio networks that pass messages without the internet or the cell network.

Since then I've put my spare hours into the mesh around Ottawa: putting up repeaters, writing the software that maps and monitors the network, helping with documentation for the Canadian community, and giving the occasional talk at local radio clubs.
